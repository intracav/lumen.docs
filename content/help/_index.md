## Top questions

- **Something's not working.** Check [service status](/developers/status/) first, then [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/).
- **"Rate limited" with a countdown.** You've reached your plan's allowance for now. Wait for the countdown, then press **Retry**. [More](/help/troubleshooting/).
- **An answer stopped partway.** It was interrupted. The partial text is kept but not finished. Press **Retry** or ask again. [More](/help/troubleshooting/).
- **My document isn't being cited.** It may still say **Indexing…**, or you can attach it to your message. [More](/help/troubleshooting/).
- **Can I trust the answers?** Check the cited sources before acting. [Hallucinations and grounding](/learn/hallucinations-and-grounding/).
- **Does Lumen de-identify what I type?** Automatically only in Lumen Research and the website assistant. [De-identification](/learn/deidentification/).
- **Which plan do I need?** [Plans](/plans/), and [intracav.ai](https://intracav.ai) for pricing.

## Contact support

Email [support@intracav.ai](mailto:support@intracav.ai). To help us answer quickly, include:

1. What you were trying to do.
2. The exact message you saw, or a screenshot with any patient information removed.
3. Roughly when it happened, and whether you were on the web, desktop or phone.
4. Your team's name if you use a team account.

{{< callout type="warning" title="Don't send patient information" >}}
Never include patient names, record numbers or other identifying details in a support email or screenshot.
{{< /callout >}}

For service-wide problems, the [status page](/developers/status/) shows current incidents.
