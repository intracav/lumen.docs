Lumen is a web app. Open [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai) in a browser on a computer, tablet or phone and sign in. Your chats, documents and settings are stored with your account, not on the device, so whatever you start in one place is waiting in the next.

## Before you start

- **A current browser,** such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
- **Your Lumen account.** Sign in with the same account on each device.
- **For Satellite:** your computer and your phone, both signed in to Lumen.

## How to use Lumen on each device

{{< tabs >}}
{{< tab name="Computer" >}}
1. Open [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai) in your browser and sign in.
2. Bookmark it, or pin the tab, so it's one click away.
3. Use the full layout: the sidebar on the left, the chat in the middle, and panels such as sources, files and **Lumen Pilot** on the right.
{{< /tab >}}
{{< tab name="Phone or tablet" >}}
1. Open [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai) in your phone's browser and sign in.
2. Add it to your home screen from the browser's share or menu button, so it opens like an app.
3. On a narrow screen, panels open as sheets from the bottom. In the message box, **Enter** adds a new line. Tap the send arrow to send.
{{< /tab >}}
{{< /tabs >}}

### Pick up where you left off

Chats and specialist work run on Lumen's servers, not in your browser. If you close the tab, lock your phone or lose signal, the work keeps going, for up to about an hour per task. Open the same chat on any device and you'll see **Lumen is still working — picking up where we left off…** while it catches up, or the finished answer.

## How to pair your phone with Lumen Satellite

*Lumen Satellite* pairs your phone with Lumen on your computer, so the phone can act as a remote. For example, you can use it as a pointer or touchpad, or approve a plan Lumen Pilot has proposed.

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="On your computer, open Live" >}}
In the sidebar, click **More**, then **Live**. Find the card **Your phone as a remote** and click **Connect**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Show the pairing code" >}}
The **Connect your phone** window shows a QR code and a typed code, with a timer (**Expires in**). If it runs out, click **New code**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Scan it with your phone" >}}
Point your phone's camera at the QR code and open the link. Sign in to Lumen if asked. Or, on your phone, open **Satellite**, enter the typed code under **Connect your phone**, and tap **Connect**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Approve the phone on your computer" >}}
If your computer asks, click **Approve**. Click **Deny** if you don't recognize the device.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Use your phone" >}}
Your phone now controls the Lumen session on your computer. The sidebar shows **Live · phone connected**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

To unpair, go back to **Live** on your computer, click **Manage** on the card, and click **Disconnect** next to the phone under **Paired devices**.

## Tips

- **Same account everywhere.** If a chat is missing on another device, check that you're signed in to the same account, and to the same workspace if you belong to a team.
- **Offline for a moment?** Messages you send while offline wait and send when you reconnect. A banner tells you how many are waiting.
- **Drafts are kept.** An unsent message stays in the box if you leave the chat and come back on the same device.

## Limits

- Lumen needs an internet connection to answer.
- A pairing code works for about 5 minutes. You can pair up to 5 devices at a time, and an unused pairing ends after about two weeks.
- Lumen Pilot runs in the app you have open. Closing the app stops Pilot, even though chat answers keep running on the server.
- Work that runs longer than about an hour stops. If a server problem interrupts an answer, Lumen marks it as interrupted and keeps the text written so far. Click **Retry** to run it again.
- Native desktop and phone apps are being prepared. Until your organization tells you otherwise, use the web app.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Do I need to install anything?" >}}
No. Lumen runs in a web browser at [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai).
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="If I close my laptop mid-answer, do I lose it?" >}}
No. The answer keeps being written on Lumen's servers. Open the chat again, on any device, to see it.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can someone else pair their phone to my session?" >}}
Only with a current code from your screen. If a device you don't recognize asks to connect, click **Deny**. You can disconnect any paired device under **Paired devices**.
{{< /faq >}}
