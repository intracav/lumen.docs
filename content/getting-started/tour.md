Lumen opens on a chat, because that's where most work starts. Around it are four other places worth knowing: **Home**, which shows what needs you; **Explore**, the list of everything Lumen can do; **Lumen Pilot**, which can show you around or do steps for you; and **Settings**. This page takes you through each one.

## Before you start

Sign in at [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai). Some items in the sidebar appear only on certain plans or for team members and admins, so your sidebar may be shorter than the one described here.

## How to find your way around

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Home" >}}
Click **Home** in the sidebar. Home greets you and shows what's waiting: **Waiting on you**, **Pick up where you left off**, **Recent work** and **At a glance**. The box at the top, **Ask Lumen anything…**, starts a new chat.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Work through the getting-started checklist" >}}
New accounts see **Get started with Lumen** on Home. It lists a few first steps, such as **Ask your first question**, **Open a citation to check it** and **Add your own knowledge**. Lumen ticks each step off when you've actually done it. Click **Ask Pilot** next to a step if you want help with it. To hide the checklist, use **Hide from Home**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Use the sidebar" >}}
The sidebar is on the left on every screen:

- **New Chat** starts a fresh conversation.
- **Home**, **Chat**, **Inbox** and **Explore** are the main places.
- **More** shows the rest, including **Workflow**, **Live**, **News** and **All history**. Some items, such as **Studio** and **Research**, appear only where they're available.
- Your recent chats are listed below, with **Pinned** chats and **Folders**.

Press {{< kbd "⌘" "B" >}} ({{< kbd "Ctrl" "B" >}} on Windows) to pin or unpin the sidebar.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Browse Explore" >}}
Click **Explore** to see every specialist, skill and built-in tool in one list. Filter by what you want to do, such as **Check medications** or **Code & bill**, or by kind: **Specialists**, **Skills** or **Built-in tools**. Click **Open** on a specialist, **Use** on a skill, or **Try it** on a tool.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open Lumen Pilot" >}}
Click the Pilot button at the bottom of the sidebar (**Lumen Pilot**), or press {{< kbd "⌘" "/" >}}. The Pilot panel opens on the right with three tabs: **Ask**, **Map** and **Help**. Ask it anything about Lumen, such as "Where do I upload a policy?" In **Show me** mode, Pilot points at things and opens screens. Where **Do it** is available, Pilot can also do the steps for you after you approve its plan.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open the feature map" >}}
In the Pilot panel, open the **Map** tab and click **See everything**. The **Feature map** lists what Lumen can do and what's available to you.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open Settings" >}}
Click your name and picture at the bottom of the sidebar (**Account and settings**). Settings has six sections: **Account**, **Plan & billing**, **Preferences**, **Appearance**, **Privacy & data** and **About & legal**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## The five places at a glance

| Place | Use it to | How to get there |
|---|---|---|
| **Chat** | Ask questions, work with a specialist, make documents | **New Chat** or **Chat** in the sidebar |
| **Home** | See what's waiting, resume work, follow the checklist | **Home** in the sidebar |
| **Explore** | Find a specialist, skill or tool | **Explore** in the sidebar |
| **Lumen Pilot** | Get help with the app, or have it do steps for you | Pilot button, or {{< kbd "⌘" "/" >}} |
| **Settings** | Change your account, plan, appearance and privacy | Your name at the bottom of the sidebar |

## Tips

- **Hid the checklist by mistake?** Open Pilot, go to **Help**, and click **Getting started** to bring it back on Home.
- **Want the welcome again?** In Pilot's **Help** tab, click **Replay the welcome**.
- **Team members see more.** If you belong to a team, you may also see **Policies**, **Learning**, and for admins, **Admin console**.

## Limits

- Pilot works in the app you have open. It isn't a background assistant, and it stops if you close the app.
- **Do it** isn't available everywhere. If you only see **Show me**, Pilot can still point you to the right place.
- What's in the sidebar depends on your plan, your team and what your organization has turned on.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Where did my old chats go?" >}}
Recent chats are in the sidebar. Everything else is under **More** > **All history**, or press {{< kbd "⌘" "K" >}} to search.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="What's the difference between a specialist, a skill and a tool?" >}}
A *specialist* is a persona you bring into a chat with `@` for multi-step work, such as Medication Review. A *skill* is a set procedure for one kind of output, such as a SOAP note, picked with `/`. A *tool* is a single lookup, such as a drug interaction check, that Lumen runs when it needs to. Explore lists all three.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Why can't I see Studio or Research?" >}}
They appear only where they're turned on. Studio is being rolled out gradually.
{{< /faq >}}
