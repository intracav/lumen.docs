Lumen is a clinical AI workspace from Intracav. You ask questions in plain words, and Lumen looks the answers up and cites where each part came from. These pages cover the basics first, then five everyday tasks you can try with a fictional example in a couple of minutes each.

## Your first 10 minutes

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Understand what Lumen is (2 minutes)" >}}
Read [What is Lumen?](/getting-started/what-is-lumen/) for the short version: who it's for, and how it keeps answers checkable.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Ask your first question (5 minutes)" >}}
Sign up at [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai), then follow [Ask your first question](/getting-started/first-chat/). Open one source and check it says what the answer says.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Take the tour (3 minutes)" >}}
[Find your way around Lumen](/getting-started/tour/): Home, the sidebar, Explore, Lumen Pilot and Settings.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}
