## Then go deeper

Once the scenarios feel natural, two paths open up:

- **[Guides](/guides/)** — step-by-step walkthroughs of bigger jobs: a prior authorization in minutes, an evidence-cited insurance appeal, a full literature review, grand-rounds slides.
- **[Learn](/learn/)** — the ideas under the hood (agents, tool use, citations, memory), explained in clinical terms. Ten minutes here makes everything else sharper.
