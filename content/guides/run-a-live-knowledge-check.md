The ICU at Harbor Valley Medical Center has a new central-line dressing policy. Before it goes into force, the educator wants to know whether the night shift understands the three things that changed. Instead of an email that nobody opens, she runs a ten-minute knowledge check at the 0700 huddle. People answer on their phones, the TV shows how the room did, and she explains the question most people missed on the spot.

This guide shows how to run that session. Your organization is called a *team* in Lumen.

## Before you start

- **A team account.** Live sessions belong to a team. If you're signed in to your personal workspace, switch to your team first. Any member of the team can start a session. See [Onboard your team](/guides/onboard-your-team/) if your organization isn't set up yet.
- **Participants.** Each person needs a phone or laptop with a browser. By default, people sign in to Lumen to join. You can also let guests without an account join (see step 6).
- **A screen in the room (optional).** Any TV or projector with a web browser works, or a laptop connected to one.
- **Your questions.** Three to five questions is plenty for a huddle. Lumen can draft them from a policy or document, and you review every one before it's added.

## How to run a live knowledge check

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Live and start a session" >}}
Sign in at [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai) and switch to your team. In the sidebar, click **Live**, then click **Start a session**.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="Name it and choose what it's about" >}}
In **Start a live session**, type a **Name**, such as "Central line dressing policy check".

Under **What it's about (optional)**, you can choose a policy, a Knowledge Pack or one of your own documents. Turn on **Let Lumen draft the agenda** if you want suggested questions, and add a note in **What would you like? (optional)**, such as "three questions on what changed, for night shift". Click **Start**.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="Add a knowledge check" >}}
If Lumen drafted questions, review each one. A drafted item shows whether it was "Quoted word for word" from your source, or "Check this before saving" when it couldn't be matched.

To add a question by hand, open the **More actions** menu and choose **Add an activity**. Choose **Knowledge check** ("One right answer, shown after"). Then:

- Type the **Question or prompt**.
- Add the answer choices, with **Add option** for each one.
- Tap the circle beside the right answer.
- Fill in **Why it is right (shown after the reveal)**. This is the teaching moment.

Leave **Scored** on if you want standings. You can mix in other activity types, such as a **Poll** or a **Scale**, to open the conversation.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="Put the session on the TV" >}}
Click **Cast to TV**. In **Show on a screen**, choose one of these:

- **On a TV:** open `/tv` in the TV's browser. It shows a code. Type that code into **Screen code** and click **Connect the TV**.
- **From this device:** click **Open in a new window** and drag it to the projector, or click **Chromecast or second screen**.

The screen shows the join code and a QR code under **Scan to join**.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="Have people join" >}}
Ask everyone to scan the QR code with their phone camera. They can also open Lumen, go to **Live**, click **Join a session**, type the code and tap **Join**.

You'll see people appear as they join.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="Decide who can join (optional)" >}}
From the **More actions** menu, open **Sharing and access**. Here you can allow **Guests without an account**, turn off **Let new people in** once everyone has arrived, set a limit, or **Replace the code** if it has been shared too widely.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="Run each question" >}}
The facilitator panel always shows one big button for the next step. For a knowledge check, it moves through:

1. **Open for answers**: phones show the question and people answer.
2. **Close answers**: answers are locked in. Nothing is shown yet.
3. **Show results to the room**: reveals the right answer, scores it, and shows your explanation on the TV and on phones.
4. **Next activity**: moves everyone to the next question.

On a keyboard, press the spacebar for the next step, **R** to reveal and **P** to pause. Press **?** for all shortcuts.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step title="End and review the results" >}}
On the last question, click **End & show final results**. The TV shows the final standings.

Open **Results** to see every answer count, the **Standings**, and any **Decisions and follow-ups** you recorded. Click **Copy results link** to share it, or **Save as template** to run the same check with another shift.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## Check it worked

- People's names (or "Player" numbers, if you hid names) appear as they join.
- After **Close answers**, the live counts stop changing.
- After **Show results to the room**, the TV and phones show the right answer and your explanation.
- The session appears under **My sessions**, and its **Results** page opens from there.

## Tips

- **Start with an easy poll.** A warm-up **Poll** gets everyone's phone connected before the questions that count.
- **Hide names for sensitive topics.** Turn on **Hide names** in **Sharing and access** so the room sees "Player 7" instead of a name.
- **Show the policy beside the question.** When the session is about a policy, you can show a section of the policy with a question, so the discussion stays tied to the actual text.
- **Reuse what works.** **Save as template** keeps the questions, so the next shift gets the same check.
- **Use your phone as a remote.** On the Live page, **Your phone as a remote** lets you advance the session from your phone while you stand at the front of the room.

## Limits and safety

- **A score is never an attestation.** Taking part in a session, or scoring well, doesn't record that someone has read or signed a policy. Policy attestation is a separate step in [qPolicy](/qpolicy/attestation/).
- **A knowledge check isn't a competency assessment.** Use it to start a conversation, not to sign off skills.
- **Sessions belong to a team.** You can't start one from a personal workspace, although anyone can join by code.
- **Live isn't video or voice.** Everyone needs to be in the room, or on a call you run separately.
- **Check drafted questions.** Lumen marks each drafted item as quoted from the source or not. Read every question and answer before you run it.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Do participants need a Lumen account?" >}}
Not if you allow it. In **Sharing and access**, turn on **Guests without an account** and people can join with just the code.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="What if the TV loses its connection?" >}}
The session keeps running. Open `/tv` on the TV again and connect it with a new screen code. Screen codes change every 10 minutes.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I see who got each question wrong?" >}}
As the host, you see every answer count. Participants see what the room saw and their own answers. If you hide names, the room sees player numbers instead.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Does this count toward CE or policy sign-off?" >}}
No. A Live score isn't a CE record or a policy attestation. Use [Learning](/learning/) for courses and CE, and [qPolicy](/qpolicy/) for attestations.
{{< /faq >}}
