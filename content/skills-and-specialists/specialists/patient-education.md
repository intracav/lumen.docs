Patients forget most of what they hear at discharge. A clear handout helps, but writing one at a sixth-grade level takes time. **Patient Education** drafts it for you: plain words, short sentences, action steps, when to call the clinic and when to call 911. Lumen measures the reading grade with a formula rather than estimating it, and traces every number, such as a dose or a weight-gain threshold, to a source.

## Before you start

- **Plan:** any plan.
- **Have ready:** the topic, who will read it (an adult patient, a parent or caregiver, or an older adult), and any instructions specific to your unit or this patient.
- **Optional:** attach your unit's existing instructions so the handout matches them.
- Patient Education writes for clinicians to give to patients. Lumen isn't a tool for patients to use directly.

## Tips

- Name the audience. A handout for a caregiver of a child reads differently from one for an older adult.
- Add your unit's specifics, such as phone numbers and clinic hours, after the draft. Lumen doesn't know them unless you tell it.
- For translations, ask for the language you need. Lumen marks a machine translation as needing review by a qualified translator before use.
- Patient Education can include a simple organ illustration to show where something is in the body. It's for explanation, not a clinical reference.

## Limits and safety

- The reading grade is a formula's measure of sentence and word length. It doesn't guarantee a patient will understand the handout. Use teach-back.
- The handout is general. Check that it fits this patient's medicines, conditions and instructions.
- Machine translations must be reviewed by a qualified translator before you give them to a patient.

{{< callout type="safety" title="Review before it reaches a patient" >}}
You're responsible for what you hand a patient. Read the whole handout, confirm every dose and warning against its source and the patient's orders, and follow your organization's patient-education policy.
{{< /callout >}}

## FAQ

{{< faq question="What reading level does it aim for?" >}}
At or below a 6th-grade reading level. The grade shown is measured from the text, never claimed.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I make a handout from our own policy?" >}}
Yes. Attach the policy or instructions and ask for a handout based on them. Lumen quotes the source for any numbers it uses.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Is this a patient-facing product?" >}}
No. Lumen is for clinicians and staff. Patient Education helps you write material that you review and give to patients.
{{< /faq >}}
