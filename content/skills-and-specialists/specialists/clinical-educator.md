Annual competencies and new-equipment in-services take hours to write, and they drift from policy over time. **Clinical Educator** builds the module from the policy itself. It writes objectives as things the learner will do, quotes the content from the policy or guideline in force, and writes questions whose right answers sit inside those quotes. If a question can't be sourced, it's removed, not kept. When you're happy with the module, you apply it as a quiz, a Live session and a learner page, and publish them yourself.

## Before you start

- **Plan:** Plus or above.
- **Workspace:** your organization's team workspace (an *organization*, called a *team* in Lumen). Switch to it in the sidebar first. Clinical Educator doesn't run in a personal workspace.
- **Have ready:** the policy or guideline to teach. It's best if it's already in your team's documents or policy library, or you can attach it.
- **Know your learners:** nurses, physicians, APPs, pharmacists, students or all staff, and the setting, such as ICU and step-down.

## How to build and apply a module

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Clinical Educator" >}}In your team workspace, click **Explore**, choose **Specialists**, click **Clinical Educator**, then **Open Clinical Educator**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Pick the task" >}}Under **Start with**, choose **Build a competency module**, **Teach a policy**, **Annual competency check** or **Course module**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Fill in the form" >}}For a competency module, enter the **Topic** (for example "central line dressing changes"), choose **Who learns** (for example **nurses**), and enter the **Setting** (for example "ICU and step-down"). For **Teach a policy**, name the **Policy** and attach it if needed.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Review the module" >}}Open the module when the task strip says **Done**. Check the verdict, each objective, and each question's quoted answer. Ask for changes in the chat, for example "Add a question on when to change a gauze dressing."{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Apply it" >}}In the module, under **Apply version**, tick **Quiz**, **Live session** and **Learner page** as needed, then click **Apply**. Lumen makes drafts. Nothing is published until you publish it.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Read completion" >}}After learners take the quiz, open the module again. **Completion** lists each learner's attempts and result, and **Items missed most** shows where to reteach.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## Tips

- Keep your policies current in Lumen. The module is only as current as the source it quotes.
- Use **Annual competency check** for skills staff must keep current, such as blood glucose monitoring.
- Turn the quiz into a Live session to run it at a huddle or skills day. See [Lumen Live](/live/).

## Limits and safety

- A passed quiz is a **knowledge check**, not a competency attestation. Record competence through your own validation process.
- Clinical Educator builds education. It doesn't write or change the policy itself.
- Applying makes drafts. The quiz, the Live session and the learner page are published by you, not by Lumen.
- If a source couldn't be read, the module lists what remains unsourced instead of filling the gap from memory.

{{< callout type="important" title="Teach from the current version" >}}
Before you publish, confirm the module quotes the version of the policy that's in force. If the policy changes, rebuild or update the module.
{{< /callout >}}

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Why does it need a team workspace?" >}}
Modules are built from your organization's own policies and become quizzes, Live sessions and learner pages for its members, so they belong to the organization's workspace.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can it make a handout for patients instead?" >}}
No. For patient handouts, use [Patient Education](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/patient-education/). Clinical Educator tells you when a request belongs there.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I use it for a nursing school course?" >}}
Yes. Choose **Course module** and enter the course and the week or topic. It writes objectives, content and assessment items from the sources you give it.
{{< /faq >}}
