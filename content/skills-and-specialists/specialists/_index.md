A **specialist** is a focused assistant inside Lumen chat. It knows which tools its job needs, which actions need your approval, and which checks its result must pass. Start one from **Explore** or type `@` in any chat. See [Work with a specialist](/skills-and-specialists/working-with-a-specialist/) for the steps.

## For clinicians

These work on every plan, in your personal workspace or a team's.

| Specialist | What you get |
|---|---|
| [**Clinical Reasoning**](/getting-started/differential/) | A checked workup: findings quoted from the case, a differential with evidence for and against, the can't-miss diagnoses. For physicians. |
| [**Medication Review**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/medication-review/) | A label-screened review: every pair checked or listed as untested, renal rules computed, proposals for the prescriber. For pharmacists and physicians. |
| [**Patient Education**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/patient-education/) | A plain-language handout with its reading grade measured, teach-back questions and sourced numbers. For nurses and patient educators. |
| [**Clinical Research**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/clinical-research/) | A reproducible evidence review: server-run searches, screened studies, quoted effects. For physicians and researchers. |
| [**Trial Matcher**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/trial-matcher/) | A registry screen per trial: criteria quoted and checked against quoted patient facts. Searching ClinicalTrials.gov needs Pro. |
| **Care Transitions** | Discharge summaries and I-PASS handoffs with completeness computed, medication changes reconciled and pending results listed. For hospitalists and nurses. |
| [**Prior Authorization**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/prior-authorization/) | A prior-auth packet for your review: payer criteria mapped to chart evidence, codes looked up. For physicians and billing staff. |
| **Document Analysis** | A checked extraction from attached documents: quoted values with locations, explicit statuses, listed conflicts. |
| **Vascular Access** | A route map, a tip-position verdict and a list of what isn't yet sourced. For vascular access nurses and PICC teams. |

## For educators and unit leaders

| Specialist | What you get | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| [**Clinical Educator**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/clinical-educator/) | A competency module from your own policies: objectives, sourced content, and quiz items whose answers are quoted. | Plus, team workspace |
| **Staff Scheduler** | A live roster grid with every rule checked, open shifts with ranked fixes, overtime and cost. See [Build a staff schedule](/guides/staff-schedule/). | Pro |

## For device makers and their teams

These run inside your organization's workspace (called a *team* in Lumen) on Lumen Max. Some also need a product that Intracav enables for your organization.

| Specialist | What you get |
|---|---|
| [**Clinical Trial Manager**](/skills-and-specialists/specialists/clinical-trial-manager/) | A device pilot run end to end: trial setup, coverage roster, training syllabus, status digests and a closeout report that waits for approval. |
| **Product Trial Manager** | Hospital product evaluations from prospect to purchase, with every outward step staged for approval. It works with ten focused helpers: **Account Research**, **Trial Planning**, **Approval Coordination**, **Trial Documentation**, **Trial Logistics**, **Clinical Education Coordinator**, **Trial Monitoring**, **Evaluation Analysis**, **Commercial Conversion** and **Executive Intelligence**. |
| **Safety Intake** | Complaints and adverse events from any channel as one quoted, clock-stamped record. A person decides reportability. |
| **Claims Review** | Marketing copy checked claim by claim against your cleared labeling or substantiation file. |
| **Calculator Builder** | A cited cost or value calculator you can publish and share. Available on any plan. |

{{< callout type="info" title="If a specialist isn't available to you" >}}
Its page says why, for example that it needs a higher plan or a team workspace, with a **See plans** button. If you type `@` for one you can't use, Lumen answers as an ordinary chat and tells you the reason. See [Plans](/plans/).
{{< /callout >}}

{{< callout type="safety" title="Specialists propose. People decide." >}}
No specialist places orders, signs, submits to a payer, or decides reportability. Review each result and its sources before you act.
{{< /callout >}}
