Every Lumen Live session is an agenda of **activities**. An activity is one question for the room, answered on phones. There are seven types. Choosing the right one is the difference between a vague show of hands and an answer you can act on.

## Before you start

You need a session to add activities to. See [Run a live session](/live/run-a-session/). A session can hold up to 40 activities.

## The seven activities

| Activity (as labelled in Lumen) | People are asked to | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Poll** | Pick one | A quick vote: "Which week works for the skills fair?" |
| **Multi-select** | Pick any that apply | "Which of these supplies run out on your shift?" |
| **Ranking** | Put them in order | Setting priorities. Results combine everyone's order into one ranking. Up to 6 options. |
| **Scale** | Rate 1 to 5 | Confidence or agreement. The room sees "Average X of 5". |
| **Open question** | Write ideas, questions or concerns | Collecting comments. You approve each answer before the room sees it. |
| **Knowledge check** | Choose the one right answer | Training. The right answer and the reason are hidden until you reveal them. |
| **Buzzer** | Buzz in first | Quiz rounds. You rule each answer correct or incorrect. |

Polls, Multi-select and Knowledge checks can have up to 8 options, of up to 120 characters each.

## How to add an activity

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open the agenda" >}}In your session, click **Add an activity**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Choose the type" >}}Pick **Poll**, **Multi-select**, **Ranking**, **Scale**, **Open question**, **Knowledge check** or **Buzzer**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Write the question" >}}Type it in **Question or prompt**. Use **Add option** for each choice.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Set the options" >}}Turn on **Anonymous** if people should answer without their names. Turn on **Show results while open** if the room should see answers arrive in real time.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Save it" >}}Click **Save**. The activity joins the agenda.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

{{< tip title="Let Lumen draft the questions" >}}
Use **Draft with Lumen** to propose activities from the session's topic or attached policy. Review each one, then choose **Add to agenda** for the ones you keep. Check knowledge-check answers yourself before you run them.
{{< /tip >}}

## Details for each activity

### Open question (moderated)

Every answer arrives as waiting. You choose **Show** or **Hide** for each one, and only shown answers reach the room. Each person can send up to 3 answers of up to 280 characters. When you're finished, click **Done with this question**.

### Knowledge check

Add the options, mark the right one, and fill in **Why it is right (shown after the reveal)**. Turn on **Scored** to award points: a right answer earns points once you reveal it. The answer stays hidden until you click **Show results to the room**.

### Buzzer

You can set **Places in the queue** (1 to 10), a **Countdown before it opens (seconds)**, **Points for a correct answer**, **Points taken for a wrong answer**, and whether a **Wrong answer sits out**. Turn on **Hide names on the screen** to show "Player 7" instead of names. For each buzz, rule **Correct**, **Incorrect** or **Set aside (no score)**. Lumen orders buzzes by when its server receives them.

### Scores and the leaderboard

Points come from buzzer rulings, scored knowledge checks and any **Adjust score** you make (with a reason, which is kept). Turn on **Leaderboard** to show the top ten on the screen and phones. It's off by default.

## Limits and safety

- **Anonymous means anonymous.** With **Anonymous** on, nobody, including you, sees who answered what. Results stay hidden until at least 3 people answer.
- **A score is not a certificate.** Knowledge-check and buzzer scores are for the room. They're never a policy attestation, a competency sign-off or continuing-education credit.
- **Check drafted questions.** Questions from **Draft with Lumen** can be wrong. Check every knowledge-check answer against the policy or source before you run it.
- **Open answers are deleted after 180 days.** Copy anything you need to keep into your meeting notes.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Which activity should I use to set priorities?" >}}
**Ranking**. Everyone orders the options, and Lumen combines the orders into one ranking for the room.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I stop an inappropriate comment from appearing?" >}}
Yes. Open questions are moderated. Nothing reaches the room until you choose **Show**.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I reuse a set of activities?" >}}
Yes. Save a finished session with **Save as template**, or build one under the **Templates** tab on the Live home page.
{{< /faq >}}
