You have twenty minutes with the unit and three things to decide. Instead of a show of hands, you open a Lumen Live session. Everyone answers on their phone, the results appear on the room's screen, and you leave with a record of the decisions.

## Before you start

- **A team account.** Sessions belong to a team, which is Lumen's word for an organization. Any member can host. If you're in a personal account, Lumen shows **Switch to a team to see or start its sessions.**
- **A screen for the room** (optional). A TV, projector or shared screen in a video call. See [Results and the TV screen](/live/results-and-tv/).
- **Your questions.** Three to six activities is plenty for a short session. See [Activity types](/live/activities/).
- **Policy sessions** need your team to have qPolicy, if you want to attach a policy.

## How to run a session

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Live" >}}In the sidebar, open **More**, then **Live**. You'll see the sessions in your team.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Start a session" >}}Click **Start a session**. Pick a purpose: **Meeting**, **Policy review**, **Training** or **Prioritization**. Give it a **Name**, such as "Q4 hand hygiene review".{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Add context (optional)" >}}Under **What it’s about (optional)**, attach a policy, a Knowledge Pack or a document the session is about. Turn on **Let Lumen draft the agenda** if you'd like Lumen to suggest activities. Click **Start**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Build the agenda" >}}Click **Add an activity**, choose a type, and type the **Question or prompt**. Use **Add option** for each answer choice. You can also use **Draft with Lumen** to propose activities, then pick the ones to keep with **Add to agenda**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Let people join" >}}Show the six-character join code or QR code on your screen. People type the code on their phones under **Join a session**. To let people without a Lumen account in, open {{< ui-path "More actions" "Sharing and access" >}} and set **Who can join** to **Anyone with the code**, then turn on **Guests without an account**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open the first question" >}}Click **Open for answers**. A live meter shows how many people have answered.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Close and show the results" >}}Click **Close answers** to lock answers in (nothing is shown yet). Then click **Show results to the room**. For a knowledge check, this reveals the right answer and scores it.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Move on" >}}Click **Next activity**. On the last one, click **End & show final results**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Record what you decided" >}}Before or after you end, use {{< ui-path "More actions" "Record an outcome" >}} to note a **Decision** or a **Follow-up**, choose **Who** owns it and set a due date.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

{{< tip title="Keyboard shortcuts" >}}
While hosting on a computer: {{< kbd "Spacebar" >}} moves to the next step, {{< kbd "R" >}} reveals, {{< kbd "P" >}} pauses, and {{< kbd "?" >}} lists every shortcut.
{{< /tip >}}

## Host controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Freezes the session. People see that it's paused. |
| **End** | Asks **End the session?** Choose **End & show results** or **Keep going**. |
| {{< ui-path "More actions" "Reveal now" >}} | Skips straight to showing results. |
| {{< ui-path "More actions" "Reopen" >}} | Opens a closed question again. |
| {{< ui-path "More actions" "Skip to next" >}} | Moves on without showing results. |
| **Make co-host** | In the people list, lets another team member run the session with you. Guests can't be co-hosts. |
| **Remove from session** | Removes a participant. |
| **Replace the code** | Issues a new join code if the old one was shared too widely. |

## Tips

- **Start with something easy.** A one-tap poll gets everyone's phone connected before the questions that matter.
- **Use anonymous mode for honest answers.** Turn on **Anonymous** for questions about workload, safety concerns or barriers. Results stay hidden until at least 3 people have answered.
- **Save what worked.** After a good session, use **Save as template** on the results page so you can run it again with another shift.

## Limits and safety

- **Hosting needs a team.** Personal accounts can join sessions by code but can't host.
- **Not video or voice.** Live handles questions and answers only. Run it alongside your room or video call.
- **Sessions end on their own.** A session nobody touches for 12 hours is ended automatically.
- **A session about a policy can't be public.** Only your team can join it.
- **A score is not an attestation.** Taking part, attendance or a knowledge-check score is never a policy acknowledgment, an attestation or a certificate. Use [qPolicy attestation](/qpolicy/) for sign-offs.
- **No patient information.** Don't put identifiable patient details in questions, and remind people not to type them into open questions.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Do participants need a Lumen account?" >}}
Not if you allow guests. By default, only members of your team can join. Open {{< ui-path "More actions" "Sharing and access" >}}, set **Who can join** to **Anyone with the code**, and turn on **Guests without an account**. Guests type a name for the session; it doesn't have to be their real one.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="How many people can join?" >}}
There's no limit by default. You can set one under **Limit (people)**, from 2 to 5,000.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can someone else run the session with me?" >}}
Yes. In the people list, choose **Make co-host** next to a team member.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Does a Live session count as policy training sign-off?" >}}
No. A Live session can be about a policy, and you can send the policy on for review from it, but taking part is never an attestation. The policy's approvers decide; nothing is approved in Live.
{{< /faq >}}
