The best Lumen Live sessions happen around one screen. Everyone answers on their phone, and the TV shows the question, the join code and the results. When the session ends, Lumen keeps the results, decisions and follow-ups on a page you can come back to or share.

## Before you start

- A Live session you're hosting. See [Run a live session](/live/run-a-session/).
- A screen with a web browser, such as a conference-room TV, a projector laptop or your own second monitor.

## How to show the session on a TV

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open the TV page on the screen" >}}On the TV's browser, go to **lumen.intracav.ai/tv**. It shows **Show a live session here** and a six-character screen code with a QR code. The code changes every 10 minutes.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open the cast options" >}}In your session, click **Cast to TV**. The **Show on a screen** panel opens.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Connect the TV" >}}Under **On a TV**, type the code into **Screen code**. Optionally give it a **Name (optional)**, such as "Boardroom TV". Click **Connect the TV**. You can also scan the TV's QR code with your phone and choose **Which session should it show?**{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Run the session" >}}The TV now follows the session: the join code, the current question, and results when you release them.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

**No TV app?** Under **From this device**, choose **Open in a new window** and drag it to a second screen, or use **Chromecast or second screen**. You can also share that window in a video call.

{{< callout type="info" title="The screen never shows your controls" >}}
Screens show what every phone in the room sees. They never show your host controls, individual answers, or names behind anonymous questions.
{{< /callout >}}

## How to review results afterwards

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open your sessions" >}}Go to **Live** and click **My sessions**. Use **All**, **Running**, **Finished** or **Archived**, and **Hosted by me**, to find it.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open the results" >}}Click the session. The results page shows each activity's answers, the scores if you used them, and the decisions and follow-ups you recorded.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Share or reuse it" >}}Use **Copy results link** to share it, **Save as template** to run it again, or **Archive** to tidy your list.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

### Who can see the results

Choose this in the session's **Results after it ends** setting:

- **Hosts**: only the people who ran the session.
- **Participants** (the default): people who took part see what the room saw, plus their own answers.
- **Anyone with link**: anyone you send the results link to.

Hosts always see every count. Anonymous answers stay anonymous.

### Decisions and follow-ups

Use {{< ui-path "More actions" "Record an outcome" >}} to note a **Decision** or a **Follow-up**. A follow-up names **Who** owns it and lands in that person's work queue with a due date. In a session about a policy, you can also send the policy for review or sign-off. Its approvers decide; nothing is approved in the session.

## Limits and safety

- **No export file.** Results live on the results page. There's no spreadsheet or PDF export.
- **Up to 12 screens** per session. A connected screen stays connected for up to 12 hours.
- **Open answers are deleted 180 days** after the session ends.
- **Results are not attestations.** The results page says it plainly: taking part in a session is not a policy acknowledgment or a certificate. For sign-offs, use [qPolicy](/qpolicy/).
- **Mind what's on the screen.** The room screen is visible to anyone in the room. Don't run sessions with identifiable patient details.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Do I need special hardware for the TV?" >}}
No. Any screen with a modern web browser can open **lumen.intracav.ai/tv**. You can also use a second monitor or Chromecast.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I show the results again after the session?" >}}
Yes. The results page keeps them, and you can share it with **Copy results link**.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can people who missed the session see the results?" >}}
If you set **Results after it ends** to **Anyone with link**, anyone with the link can see them. Otherwise only participants or hosts can.
{{< /faq >}}
