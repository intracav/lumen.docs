**Lumen Live** runs real-time sessions for a group in the same room or on the same call. You prepare a short agenda of questions, such as a poll, a ranking or a knowledge check. People join with a six-character code on their phones, and the answers appear on a TV or shared screen as they come in. Live is text and taps only: it isn't a video or voice-call tool.

## Good to know

- **Hosting needs a team.** Sessions belong to a team (Lumen's word for an organization). Any member can start one. In a personal account you can still join sessions by code.
- **Taking part is not a sign-off.** A Live answer or score is never a policy acknowledgment, an attestation or a certificate. Use [qPolicy](/qpolicy/) for attestations and [Lumen Learning](/learning/) for course records.
- **Not video or voice.** Run Live alongside your meeting room or video call.
