Lumen covers chat, specialists, knowledge, workflows, Live sessions, policies and more. The **feature map** puts all of it on one page. Each entry says what that part of Lumen is for, who it's for and whether you can use it right now. You open the feature map from **Lumen Pilot**, the assistant for the Lumen app.

The feature map tells you where things are. To get a task done, such as reviewing a medication list or drafting a handout, start from [Explore](/skills-and-specialists/explore/) instead.

## Before you start

- **Sign in to Lumen** on the web or in the desktop app.
- **Pick the right workspace.** Some features belong to a team. If you're in your personal workspace, switch to your team in the sidebar to see what your team has.

## How to open the feature map

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Lumen Pilot" >}}Click the Pilot dot on the screen, or press {{< kbd "⌘" "/" >}} (Ctrl + / on Windows) or {{< kbd "F1" >}}.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Go to the Map tab" >}}In the **Lumen Pilot** panel, select **Map**. You'll see a short list of features you can search.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open the full map" >}}Select **See everything**. The **Feature map** page opens.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Narrow the list" >}}Type in **Search features**, or choose a filter: **For you**, **All**, or a group such as **Answers & evidence** or **Policies & compliance**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Open a feature" >}}Select any feature to see its details. Then choose **Open** and the feature's name to go there, **Ask Pilot about this**, or **Read the docs**.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

### The groups

Features are grouped by what they're for: **Answers & evidence**, **Your knowledge**, **Work & automation**, **Learning & growth**, **Team setup**, **Policies & compliance**, and **Assistants & public pages**. **For you** shows only what you can use now. **All** shows everything, including features you can't open yet.

### What each status means

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Available** | You can open and use it now. |
| **Preview** | You can use it, but it's still changing. |
| **Needs setup** | Someone has to set it up first. For admins, a setup step is waiting. |
| **Not available here** | You can't use it in this workspace. The map says why in plain words, for example that it needs a team admin or owner. |
| **Coming soon** | Announced but not ready. It can't be opened. |

### How it fits together

Below the list, **How it fits together** draws Lumen as three columns: **Knowledge** (what Lumen draws on), **Lumen answers** (where it's put to work) and **Where people use it** (beyond the app, such as website assistants). Select any part for details.

### A feature's details

Each feature's page has the same sections: **Why it matters**, **Who it is for**, **Before you start**, **How to start**, **Tips** and **Related**.

## Tips

- **Use For you first.** It hides what you can't use, so the list stays short.
- **Admins: look for Needs setup.** It's a quick way to see which team features still need a setup step.
- **New features are marked.** A feature added recently shows as new until you open it.
- **Ask Pilot when you're stuck.** **Ask Pilot about this** opens Pilot with a question about that feature.

## Limits and safety

- **The map shows; it doesn't grant.** Opening a feature still checks your plan and your role. A status can't give you access you don't have.
- **Statuses need a connection.** If Lumen can't confirm your availability, the map hides the statuses rather than guess. You can still open features where you have access.
- **It's per workspace.** Your personal workspace and your team can show different statuses for the same feature.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="What's the difference between the feature map and Explore?" >}}
The feature map is a guide to the whole app: every area and whether it's open to you. [Explore](/skills-and-specialists/explore/) is where you start work: it lists the specialists, skills and tools you can use in a chat.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Why is a feature I expected marked Not available here?" >}}
It may need a team workspace, a team admin or a different plan. The map gives the reason. If you belong to a team, switch to it in the sidebar and check again. See [Plans](/plans/) for what each plan includes.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I open the feature map without Pilot?" >}}
Pilot is the way in. Open Pilot, choose **Map**, then **See everything**.
{{< /faq >}}
