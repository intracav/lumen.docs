When you're presenting from Lumen, for example running a Live knowledge check on a unit TV, the keyboard is often across the room. **Lumen Satellite** turns your phone into a remote for the Lumen screen on your computer. You can move a pointer, press buttons, and approve or cancel a **Lumen Pilot** plan from your hand. Pilot is the assistant that can do steps in the app for you after you approve its plan.

## Before you start

- **Two devices, one account.** Open Lumen on your computer (the big screen) and on your phone. Sign in to the same Lumen account on both.
- **A camera helps.** Scanning the QR code is quickest. You can type the code instead.
- **Up to five pairings.** One account can have up to 5 paired sessions at a time. Disconnect old ones you no longer use.

## How to pair your phone

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open the pairing panel on your computer" >}}In the sidebar, open {{< ui-path "More" "Live" >}}. On the card **Your phone as a remote**, select **Connect**. A panel titled **Scan with your phone** shows a QR code and a typed code, with a countdown such as **Expires in 4:52**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Scan the code with your phone" >}}Point your phone's camera at the QR code and open the link.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Or type the code" >}}On your phone, open Lumen and go to **Live**. On the card **Use this phone as a remote**, tap **Open**, then type the code from your computer.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Approve a typed code on the computer" >}}If you typed the code, your computer asks you to **Approve** or **Deny** the device. Approve only a phone you're holding: it will be able to control what's on that screen.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Start using the remote" >}}Once paired, the computer shows a short tour of what the phone can do. Select **Done**. The sidebar now reads **Live · phone connected**.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

Codes last 5 minutes. If yours runs out, the panel says **Expired**. Select **New code** and try again.

## How to approve a Pilot plan from your phone

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Ask Pilot for something on the computer" >}}For example: "Open the sepsis knowledge check and show the results on screen."{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Check the plan on the big screen" >}}Pilot shows its **Lumen plan** on the computer.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Answer on your phone" >}}On your phone, the **Lumen Pilot** card reads **Waiting for your approval**. Tap **Run** to approve the plan the computer is showing, or **Cancel**. While it runs, tap **Stop** to end it.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

On the phone, Satellite has three tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Controls** | Buttons for what's open on the computer, plus **On this screen**: every control on that page. Tap a row to point at it on the big screen; tap **Select** to press it, flip it or type in it. |
| **Remote** | A touchpad (drag to move, tap to click, two fingers to scroll) and a laser pointer (aim the phone, tap **Set centre**, pick a colour). |
| **Work** | Your queue for this team, at hand. |

## Tips

- **Present from your hand.** Pair before the session starts, then run a Live session from the front of the room.
- **Close the panel from the phone.** While the pairing panel is on the big screen, your phone shows a banner with **Close**. The link stays up.
- **Check your paired devices.** The pairing panel lists **Paired devices**, each **Online** or **Paired**, with **Disconnect**.

## Limits and safety

- **The phone controls the screen it's paired with.** Anyone holding your paired phone can press controls on that computer. Approve only a phone you're holding, and disconnect phones you've lent or lost.
- **Lumen relays; it doesn't act on its own.** Lumen's server passes messages between the two devices. The actions happen in the app on your computer, under your account and permissions.
- **Pilot's limits still apply.** Approving from the phone is the same as pressing **Run** on the computer. Controls Pilot never presses, such as delete, publish or sign, stay blocked.
- **Pairing codes expire after 5 minutes.** Generate a new code if yours runs out.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Does my phone need its own Lumen account?" >}}
No. Sign in to the same Lumen account on the phone and the computer.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="How do I unpair my phone?" >}}
Open the pairing panel on your computer ({{< ui-path "More" "Live" >}}, then **Manage** on the **Your phone is connected** card) and select **Disconnect** next to the phone.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I pair more than one phone?" >}}
Yes, up to 5 paired sessions per account.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I approve Pilot plans from my phone when I'm away from the computer?" >}}
The phone approves the plan the computer is showing. Pilot still runs in the Lumen app open on that computer, so the app has to stay open there.
{{< /faq >}}
