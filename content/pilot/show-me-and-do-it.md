Lumen has a lot of screens: Live sessions, workflows, knowledge, policies, settings. When you aren't sure where something is, ask **Lumen Pilot**. Pilot reads the screen you're on and answers in plain language. If you allow it, Pilot can also do the steps for you, such as creating a Live knowledge check, after you've looked over its plan and pressed **Run**.

Pilot has two levels, and you choose the ceiling:

- **Show me**: "Pilot answers, points and opens screens. It never changes anything."
- **Do it**: "Pilot can also do the work in the app. You approve its plan first."

Even at **Do it**, a "how do I…" question is still answered by showing you the way.

## Before you start

- **Have Lumen open.** Pilot works in the app in front of you, on the web at [lumen.intracav.ai](https://lumen.intracav.ai) or in the desktop app.
- **Know what you want to do.** Plain words are fine: "Set up a knowledge check on sepsis for the ICU."
- **You can only do what your account allows.** Pilot uses your own permissions. If a screen needs a team admin, Pilot can't open it for you either.

## How to ask Pilot to show you

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Lumen Pilot" >}}On a computer, click the Pilot dot on the screen, or press {{< kbd "⌘" "/" >}} (Ctrl + / on Windows), or press {{< kbd "F1" >}}. On a phone, tap the Pilot bar. The **Lumen Pilot** panel opens on the **Ask** tab.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Check the level" >}}Under **How far Pilot may go**, make sure **Show me** is selected.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Ask your question" >}}Type in the **Reply or ask Lumen…** box, or tap a suggestion such as **What can I do here?** or **Show me around**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Follow the pointer" >}}Pilot answers, points at the controls you need and can open the right screen. You do the clicking.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

To ask about one part of the screen, tap **Point at something on screen**, then select it. Pilot answers about that control.

## How to let Pilot do it

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Switch to Do it" >}}In the **Lumen Pilot** panel, under **How far Pilot may go**, select **Do it**. Lumen remembers your choice on this device.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Say what you want done" >}}For example: "Create a Live knowledge check on sepsis recognition for the Harbor Valley ICU, five questions."{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Review the plan" >}}Pilot shows a **Lumen plan** card headed **Review before Lumen runs this**. It lists each step it will take. The card says **Needs your OK — nothing runs until you press Run**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Choose what to approve" >}}Press **Run** to approve this plan only. If the task needs more than one plan, **Run and allow the rest of this task** approves the remaining plans for this task. **Approve all this session** approves Pilot's plans until you end the session. Press **Cancel** to stop before anything happens.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Watch it work" >}}Pilot moves through the app where you can see it and describes each step. Press **Stop** at any time. When it finishes, the card reads **Lumen worked in the app**.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## Tips

- **Start with Show me.** It's the default, and it's the fastest way to learn where things are.
- **Be specific.** Name the screen, the team or the title you want. Pilot asks when something is unclear.
- **Ask about what you're looking at.** "What is this for?" works on any screen.
- **Keep the panel open while it works.** You'll see each step, and you can press **Stop**.

## Limits and safety

- **Pilot isn't a background worker.** It works in the app you have open. If you close the app or the tab, Pilot stops. For long work that keeps going on Lumen's servers, use a [specialist](/skills-and-specialists/working-with-a-specialist/).
- **Some controls are always off limits.** Pilot won't press controls that delete or remove, publish or unpublish, pay, buy, subscribe, upgrade or downgrade, transfer ownership, sign or sign off, approve, revoke, deactivate, close an account or erase data. It also won't sign, attest or record a medication as given. These are checked in the app and again on Lumen's server, so a plan that includes one is refused. You do those steps yourself.
- **Approving a plan doesn't widen your access.** Pilot can only do what you can do. A wider approval, such as **Approve all this session**, never goes beyond what you approved.
- **Do it may not be offered.** If your account doesn't have **Do it**, Pilot stays at **Show me**.
- **Check the result.** When Pilot finishes, look over what it set up before you share or start it.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Can Pilot change something without asking me?" >}}
No. At **Show me**, Pilot never changes anything. At **Do it**, every plan waits for you to press **Run**, unless you chose **Run and allow the rest of this task** or **Approve all this session** for that task or session.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can Pilot delete things or publish for me?" >}}
No. Deleting, publishing, paying, signing and approving are blocked for Pilot, even if you ask. You do those yourself.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="What if I switch screens while Pilot is working?" >}}
Pilot's progress follows you. A small Pilot bar shows what it's doing, with **Stop**, and a plan waiting for you can be approved from there.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Does Pilot answer clinical questions?" >}}
Pilot is for using the Lumen app. Ask clinical questions in a chat, where answers cite their sources. See [Ask your first question](/getting-started/first-chat/).
{{< /faq >}}
