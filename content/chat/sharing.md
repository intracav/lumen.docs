You worked through a central line maintenance question with Lumen and want your unit educator to see it, sources and all. Share the chat. Lumen makes a read-only link. You choose who can open it and what it shows, such as the sources, and whether people can carry the conversation on as their own copy.

## Before you start

- **Plan:** any plan. Sharing only with your team needs a team account. (Lumen calls an organization a *team*.)
- **Re-read the chat.** Everything you typed is visible to whoever opens the link. Remove anything that could identify a patient first.

## How to share a chat

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open the share sheet" >}}
In the chat, click **Share** under Lumen's latest reply. On a phone, you can also tap the share icon at the top of the chat. The **Share Chat** sheet opens.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Choose who can open it" >}}
Under **Visibility**, choose:
- **Anyone with the link**: no sign-in required.
- **Signed-in users only**: viewers need a Lumen account.
- **Team members**: only members of your team.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Choose what to include" >}}
Under **Include in shared view**, turn on or off **Sources & references**, **Thinking steps** and **Tool call details**. Turn on **Let viewers continue this chat as their own copy** if you want people to be able to carry it on.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Create and copy the link" >}}
Click **Create Share Link**, then **Copy share link**. Paste it into an email or message.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## How to stop sharing

Open the **Share Chat** sheet again and click **Revoke share link**. The link stops working straight away. Copies that people made before you revoked it are theirs, and stay with them.

## What the person you share with sees

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="They open the link" >}}
They see the conversation as it happened, with whatever you chose to include. They can read it, but they can't type into your chat.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="They continue it, if you allowed that" >}}
They click **Continue this conversation**. Lumen copies the chat into their own account as a new chat. Without an account, they see **Sign up to continue this chat** or **Sign in**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

Nothing they do in their copy changes your original.

## Share inside your team without a link

In a team workspace, you can also set a chat's audience under **Audience in your team**: **Personal** (just you), **Department** or **Team**. Colleagues with access can open it in Lumen and click **Fork to continue** to carry it on as their own copy. Only you can add to the original.

## Tips

- **Include the sources.** A shared clinical answer without its sources is hard for the reader to check.
- **Use Team members for internal work.** It keeps the link inside your organization.
- **Share the chat to share a file.** Files Lumen made, such as a handout, are part of the conversation.

## Limits and safety

- **A shared link isn't a live group chat.** Each person who continues it gets a separate copy.
- **Anyone with the link** means anyone. Treat it like an email attachment you can't recall once it's been read.
- Revoking a link stops new views. It doesn't remove copies people already made.

{{< callout type="warning" title="Check for patient information before you share" >}}
Everything you typed is visible to the people who open the link. Remove names, dates of birth, record numbers and other identifiers first, and follow your organization's rules on sharing.
{{< /callout >}}

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Can the person I share with change my chat?" >}}
No. They can read it, and continue it as their own copy if you allowed that. Your original is unchanged.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Do they need a Lumen account?" >}}
Not to read a link set to **Anyone with the link**. They need an account to open a **Signed-in users only** link, or to continue the chat.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="How do I stop a link from working?" >}}
Open **Share Chat** for that chat and click **Revoke share link**.
{{< /faq >}}
