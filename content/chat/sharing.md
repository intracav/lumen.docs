Had a conversation worth showing a colleague? A well-researched answer, a differential you worked through, a protocol comparison? You can share any chat as a **read-only link**. The recipient sees the conversation exactly as it happened — and if they want to keep going, they can fork it into their own account.

## Creating a share link

Open the chat you want to share and choose **Share**. Lumen creates a unique link you can copy and send by email, message, or however you normally reach your colleagues.

When you create the link you choose who can open it:

- **Anyone with the link** — no Lumen account needed; the link itself is the key
- **Signed-in users only** — the viewer must be logged in to Lumen
- **Your organization only** — restricted to members of your organization

You also choose whether recipients are allowed to **fork** the chat (on by default).

## What recipients see

Recipients see the conversation transcript — your questions and Lumen's answers, in order, just as you saw them. They can read and scroll, but they cannot type into your chat.

{{< callout type="important" >}}
**Sharing is read-only.** Lumen does not have live group chat — a share link is a window into a finished conversation, not a shared workspace. If a recipient wants to continue the conversation, they fork it, and the fork is **their own private copy**. Nothing they do in their copy touches your original.
{{< /callout >}}

## Forking a shared chat

If you've received a share link and want to pick up where the conversation left off, choose **Fork**. This copies the conversation into your own account as a new chat that you own — you can then ask follow-up questions, attach your own documents, and take it in any direction.

Forking requires a Lumen account (the copy needs an owner), and it's only available when the person who shared the link allowed it.

## Revoking a link

You stay in control after sharing. From the chat's share settings you can see every link you've created, change who can open one, or **revoke** it entirely. A revoked link stops working immediately — anyone who opens it afterward sees nothing. Forks that were made while the link was active remain with their owners, since those are independent copies.

## Sharing inside your organization

Share links aren't the only way to make a chat visible to others. If you belong to an organization, you can also change a chat's visibility so it appears to your **department** or your whole **organization** directly in Lumen — no link required. Colleagues with access can read it and fork it to continue on their own; only you can add messages to the original.

## Before you share: think PHI

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
**A share link contains the conversation itself.** Everything you typed — including any patient details — is visible to whoever opens the link. Before sharing, re-read the conversation as if you were the recipient, and de-identify anything that could identify a patient. See [Privacy & De-identification](/tools/privacy/) for how Lumen helps you strip identifiers from clinical text.
{{< /callout >}}
