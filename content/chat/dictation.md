A spoken question is often fuller than a typed one. "Sixty-eight-year-old on apixaban, dental extraction tomorrow, do we hold it?" takes five seconds to say. Start a voice conversation in any chat, speak, and Lumen answers aloud, with captions on screen. The conversation stays in the chat, so you can read it back, check its sources, and carry on typing.

## Before you start

- **A microphone.** Your computer's built-in microphone or a headset works.
- **Browser permission.** The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. Click **Allow**.
- **A private space, if you're discussing a case.** People nearby can hear you and Lumen.

## How to talk to Lumen

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Start voice" >}}
In a chat, click the microphone in the message box (**Voice conversation**). The box changes to the voice controls and shows **Starting…**.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Speak" >}}
When you see **Speak when you're ready**, ask your question in your normal voice. Your words appear in the box as you speak. Lumen shows **Listening…** while you talk.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Pause and listen" >}}
When you stop speaking, Lumen shows **Thinking…**, then answers aloud. The answer appears as captions above the box. Click **Hide captions** if you don't want them.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Keep talking, or mute" >}}
Ask a follow-up when Lumen finishes. Click **Mute** to stop Lumen hearing you, and **Unmute** to carry on.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="End the conversation" >}}
Click **End**. The message box returns. What you said and Lumen's answers stay in the chat, with their sources.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## Tips

- **Speak in whole phrases** and pause at the end of your question. Lumen uses the pause to know you've finished.
- **Spell out tricky drug names** if Lumen mishears them, or correct them by typing after you end voice.
- **Read it back.** Open the chat afterwards to read the answer and check the sources.
- **Pilot listens too.** You can also talk to Lumen Pilot in its panel to get help with the app.

## Limits and safety

- **Speech can be misheard.** Sound-alike drug names are the most common problem. Check what Lumen heard in the chat before relying on the answer.
- **Voice doesn't replace your dictation system.** It's a conversation with Lumen, not a transcription service for your record.
- **Saying it is the same as typing it.** It's easy to say a patient's name out of habit. Don't say anything you wouldn't type.
- **Voice needs a steady connection.** If the connection drops, Lumen shows **Reconnecting…** and carries on when it's back.

{{< callout type="warning" title="Don't say patient identifiers" >}}
Spoken words are turned into text and kept in the chat. Leave out names, dates of birth and record numbers, just as you would when typing. See [Privacy and data](/trust/privacy/).
{{< /callout >}}

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Can I use voice to dictate a note?" >}}
You can describe the visit out loud and ask Lumen to draft the note, then review it as text. Voice in Lumen is a conversation, so it isn't a word-for-word transcription service. See [Draft a clinical note](/getting-started/draft-a-note/).
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Is the conversation saved?" >}}
Yes. What you said and Lumen's answers are saved in the chat, like typed messages.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Why isn't the microphone working?" >}}
Check that your browser has permission to use the microphone, and that no other app is using it. Then click **Voice conversation** again.
{{< /faq >}}
