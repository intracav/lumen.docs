A Lumen chat works like a conversation with a well-read colleague who shows their sources. You type, or talk, and Lumen looks things up, answers, and cites where each part came from. It can also hand you a checklist, a calculator, a chart or a finished document. These pages explain each part, in the order you'll meet them.
