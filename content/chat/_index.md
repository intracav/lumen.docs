If you've used iMessage or WhatsApp, you already know how to use Lumen. Your messages go on the right. Lumen's answers appear on the left. The guides below walk you through the details &mdash; like how to read the sources, what those cards below the answer are, and how to upload a document for Lumen to read. They also cover what happens after an answer lands: editing a question you already sent, sharing a conversation with a colleague, and what Lumen remembers about you between chats.
