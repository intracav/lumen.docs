Lumen has four plans: **Free** and **Pro** for individuals, **Plus** and **Max** for organizations. Every plan gets the same clinical answers with the same citations — plans differ in how much you can use, which tools come along, and what your team can share. For current prices, see [intracav.ai](https://intracav.ai/).

## Which plan am I?

- **You're a clinician trying Lumen out** — start on **Free**. Real clinical lookups, real citations, no card required.
- **You use Lumen as part of your working day** — **Pro**. Higher limits, the full tool library, and the browser extension {{< status-badge "beta" >}}.
- **You run a department** — **Plus**. Seats for your team, shared [Folders and Departments](/knowledge/), and an EHR connection.
- **You run a hospital or health system — or you need [Knowledge Packs](/knowledge/knowledge-packs/), [Website Bots](/organizations/), or [Policies](/organizations/)** — **Max**. Everything in Plus, plus the organization-wide content and publishing layer.

Limits use a **rolling window**, not a daily reset — think of it like a drip rate rather than a bolus. If you hit the ceiling, older messages age out of the window and capacity comes back on its own.

## Seats on organization plans

Plus and Max are billed per seat, and both include **5 seats** to start. From there:

- **Seats grow with your team.** When you admit a member beyond your purchased seats, the count expands automatically — nobody gets locked out mid-onboarding.
- **Seats never shrink silently.** When someone leaves, you keep the seat until an admin explicitly reduces the count, so invoices stay predictable.
- **Self-serve covers up to 25 seats.** Above 25 seats — or if your organization needs SSO or a signed BAA — that's an Enterprise conversation: [talk to sales](https://intracav.ai/).

Usage on organization plans is **pooled**: every seat adds 600 (Plus) or 1,000 (Max) messages per rolling window to one shared allowance. A colleague's quiet week is capacity your heaviest user can draw on — the pool scales with the team instead of walling each person in.

## Billing questions

{{< faq question="Can I try Lumen free?" >}}
Yes. The Free plan needs no payment details and includes the core clinical toolset — drug interactions, coding lookups, literature search, and document exports — with 50 messages every rolling 4 hours. Start with the [getting started scenarios](/getting-started/) and upgrade only when you're bumping into the ceiling.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq question="What happens when I hit my message limit?" >}}
Lumen tells you, and nothing is lost — your chats and documents stay right where they are. Because limits use a rolling window rather than a midnight reset, capacity returns gradually as older messages age out. If you're hitting the limit regularly, that's usually the sign you've outgrown your plan — see the [comparison table](/plans/) above.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq question="Do members of my organization need their own subscription?" >}}
No. A seat on a Plus or Max plan covers everything a member needs — they sign in and get the organization's full capability. If a member also holds a personal subscription, Lumen simply applies whichever is more capable; nobody is ever downgraded by joining an organization.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq question="How do seats work?" >}}
Both organization plans include 5 seats, and each additional member adds one. Seats expand automatically when you admit someone past your purchased count, and only shrink when an admin explicitly reduces them. Self-serve billing covers up to 25 seats; beyond that, [talk to sales](https://intracav.ai/).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq question="What is the Policies (qPolicy) add-on?" >}}
Policies is a paid add-on for Max organizations that manages your policy library as a governed, first-class source — so Lumen's answers can cite your own institutional policy the way they cite the literature. Policies seats are held by members of your Max organization. Learn more in [For Organizations](/organizations/).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq question="What if we need SSO or a BAA?" >}}
Single sign-on and signed Business Associate Agreements are handled through Enterprise agreements rather than self-serve checkout — a BAA is a legal document, not a checkbox. [Talk to sales](https://intracav.ai/) and we'll set it up properly. See [Trust & Security](/trust/) for how Lumen is built with HIPAA safeguards.
{{< /faq >}}
