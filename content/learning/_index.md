**Lumen Learning** brings together the courses you take, the licenses you keep current and the records you earn. Educators can write courses from evidence, send them for review, publish them and assign them to their team. Separately, the **Cert Binder** keeps every quiz you take in Lumen, with a downloadable proof PDF.

{{< callout type="important" title="Courses and license tracking are being rolled out" >}}
Courses, assignments and license tracking are switched on gradually. If you don't see **Learning** in the sidebar, it isn't on for your account yet. The **Cert Binder** is available now from your profile.
{{< /callout >}}

## Good to know

- **Records say what they are.** A completion record from a course that isn't accredited says so plainly: it isn't accredited continuing-education credit. Your board or employer decides what counts.
- **Lumen doesn't check your board's rules.** License tracking uses the requirement you enter.
- **A score isn't an attestation.** Quiz and course results are learning records, never policy sign-offs. Use [qPolicy](/qpolicy/) for attestations.
- **Team** is Lumen's word for an organization.
