Your unit has a competency day next week on the revised CLABSI prevention bundle. Ask Lumen for a ten-question quiz on it. Lumen writes the questions, grades every answer and explains each one. Each scored attempt is saved to your **Cert Binder**, your personal record of quiz attempts, with a proof PDF you can download.

## Before you start

- **A Lumen account.** Quizzes and the Cert Binder are available on every plan.
- **A topic or source.** A guideline, a policy you've uploaded, a [Knowledge Pack](/knowledge/knowledge-packs/) or a plain topic such as "midline catheter care".

## How to take a quiz

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open your Cert Binder" >}}Go to **Settings**, then **Cert binder**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Start a new quiz" >}}Click **New quiz**. Under **What should Lumen quiz you on?**, type the topic, such as "CLABSI prevention bundle". Choose how many **Questions**, or pick a suggestion under **Or pick one**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Take it" >}}Click **Start quiz**. The quiz opens in a chat. Answer each question.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Read the explanations" >}}Lumen grades every answer and explains why the right answer is right, so a wrong answer tells you what to re-read.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Find it in your Cert Binder" >}}The attempt is saved automatically. Open it in your Cert Binder and click **Download proof PDF**.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

You can also ask for a quiz in any chat, for example: "Quiz me with 10 questions on our CLABSI bundle. Passing score 80%." The pass mark is 70% unless you set another.

Each attempt is kept separately and numbered, so retakes show your progress instead of overwriting it. Opening an attempt shows its **Proof Packet**: every question, your answer, the rationale, and an **Integrity fingerprint**, a code that changes if the record is altered.

## Retakes

From an attempt, choose **Retake** for the same questions, **Retake with new questions** for a fresh set on the same topic, or **Try a harder quiz**.

## Tips

- **Quiz from your own source.** Name the policy or document, and Lumen bases the questions on it. Answers from your own material are more useful than a general quiz.
- **Study first.** Ask Lumen for a study guide before the quiz, then use the quiz to check yourself.
- **For a whole room,** run the questions as a [Live knowledge check](/live/activities/) instead.

## Limits and safety

- **A quiz is a knowledge check.** It's never a competency attestation, a policy sign-off or accredited continuing-education credit. Your educator or employer decides whether to accept it.
- **Your Cert Binder is personal.** It holds your own quiz attempts. You can download each proof PDF and delete attempts. It doesn't store uploaded certificates; see [Track licenses and education](/learning/ce-tracking/).
- **Questions can be wrong.** Lumen writes the questions and answers. If an answer disagrees with your policy or the source, follow the source and tell your educator.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Can my manager see my quiz results?" >}}
The Cert Binder is your personal record. You choose whether to share a proof PDF.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Does a passed quiz count as CE credit?" >}}
No. It's a record of your quiz attempt, not accredited continuing-education credit.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I delete an attempt?" >}}
Yes. Open the attempt and delete it. Lumen asks **Delete attempt?** to confirm.
{{< /faq >}}
