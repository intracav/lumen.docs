Your educator has assigned a course on the updated central-line dressing policy, and it's due before the next audit. Lumen Learning shows it at the top of your home page. You work through the lessons, take the assessment and finish with a completion record you can download.

{{< callout type="important" title="Being rolled out" >}}
Lumen Learning is being switched on gradually. If you don't see **Learning** in the sidebar, it isn't on for your account yet.
{{< /callout >}}

## Before you start

- **Learning switched on** for your account.
- **A course to take.** Either your team has published or assigned one, or you can **Browse courses** open to any Lumen account.

## How to take a course

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Learning" >}}Click **Learning** in the sidebar. The home page shows **Needs your attention**, **In progress**, **Assigned to you** and **Your records**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Choose a course" >}}Open a course under **Assigned to you**, or click **Browse courses** to look through the catalog.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Work through the lessons" >}}Read each lesson in order. Lessons can include text, key points and cases.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Take the assessment" >}}Answer the questions. The course page shows the pass mark and how many attempts you have.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Complete the evaluation" >}}Answer the short course evaluation.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Get your record" >}}Your completion record appears under **Your records**. Open **My records** to download the **Certificate (PDF)** or **Copy verification link**.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

{{< tip title="Stuck on a lesson?" >}}
Use **Ask the tutor** to open a new chat about the course, and ask Lumen to explain a point in another way.
{{< /tip >}}

## Assignments

When your team assigns a course, it appears under **Assigned to you**. Its status shows as **Not started**, **In progress** or **Completed** with the date. Your team's admins see the same statuses and dates, not your answers.

## Limits and safety

- **Being rolled out.** Learning isn't on for every account yet.
- **Not every record is accredited credit.** If a course isn't accredited, its record says: "This is a record of completion. It is not accredited continuing education credit." Check with your board or employer whether they accept it.
- **A course record isn't a policy attestation.** Completing a course about a policy doesn't sign you off on that policy. Use [qPolicy](/qpolicy/) for attestations.
- **Courses can contain mistakes.** Course authors review their content, but follow your organization's current policy where they differ.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Who can see my results?" >}}
Your team's admins see whether you've started or finished an assigned course, and when. They see status and dates, not your answers.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Does a Lumen course count toward my license renewal?" >}}
Only if the course is accredited and your board accepts it. Non-accredited records say so plainly. Your board decides which hours it accepts.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I take courses outside my team?" >}}
Yes, if an author made a course open to **Anyone with a Lumen account**. Find them with **Browse courses**.
{{< /faq >}}
