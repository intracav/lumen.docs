A policy has changed, and every nurse on the unit needs to know what's different. As an educator, you can build a short course in Lumen Learning: objectives, lessons, an assessment and an evaluation. A colleague reviews it, you publish it, and you assign it to your team. Lumen then shows who has started and finished.

{{< callout type="important" title="Being rolled out" >}}
Lumen Learning is being switched on gradually, and a team must be set up as a learning provider by Intracav. If you see **This team is not set up as a learning provider. Intracav turns that on.**, contact your Intracav representative.
{{< /callout >}}

## Before you start

- **A learning-provider team.** Intracav turns this on for your team (Lumen's word for an organization).
- **A role.** Team owners and admins are **Provider admin** automatically. Others need the **Educator**, **Reviewer** or **Compliance** role.
- **A second person to review.** The reviewer must be someone other than the author.
- **Your sources.** The policy, guideline or evidence the course is based on.

## How to write and publish a course

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open course authoring" >}}On your Learning home page, open **Course authoring for** your team. Click **New course**, enter a **Title** and click **Create**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Write the content" >}}In the **Content** section, add **Learning objectives**, then **Lessons**. Each lesson is made of **Text**, **Key point** and **Case** blocks.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Build the assessment" >}}Under **Assessment**, add questions (**One correct** or **Several correct**), set the **Pass mark (%)** and the **Attempts allowed**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Add the evaluation and faculty" >}}Fill in the **Learner evaluation** and **Authors and faculty**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Choose who can take it" >}}Under **Who can take it**, choose **Members of this team** or **Anyone with a Lumen account**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Check the evidence" >}}Open **Review and publish** and click **Check evidence** to see how the course's statements line up with its sources.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Send for review" >}}Click **Send for review**. The reviewer reads it and chooses **Approve this review** or **Request changes**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Approve and publish" >}}When the review is approved, click **Approve**, then **Publish**. The course status changes to **Published**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Assign it" >}}In **Learners**, click **Assign to everyone** and confirm. Then open **Assigned learning** to see who has **Not started**, is **In progress**, or has **Completed** it.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

{{< tip title="Start from a Clinical Educator module" >}}
If you've already built an education module with the Clinical Educator specialist in chat, choose **Create a course from this module** to start the course from it.
{{< /tip >}}

## Course statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being written. Only authors see it. |
| **In review** | Sent for review. |
| **Approved** | Review approved, ready to publish. |
| **Published** | Learners can take it. |
| **Replaced** | A newer version has been published. |
| **Withdrawn** | Taken out of use. |

To change a published course, choose **Start a new version**. Use **Withdraw** to take a course out of use, or **Return to draft** before it's published.

## Limits and safety

- **Being rolled out.** Learning and learning-provider setup aren't on for every team yet.
- **Assigning goes to everyone.** In the app, you can currently assign a course to everyone in your team, without a due date.
- **Who sees reports.** Team owners and admins can assign and read the **Assigned learning** report. It shows status and dates only, not answers.
- **Accredited credit needs authorization.** Records from a course are non-accredited completion records unless Intracav has recorded your organization's authorization as an accredited provider.
- **Clinical content must be right.** Have a qualified reviewer check every statement against the current policy and its sources. Lumen's evidence check helps, but doesn't replace the review.
- **Not an attestation.** Finishing a course about a policy isn't a policy sign-off. Use [qPolicy](/qpolicy/) for attestations.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Can I review my own course?" >}}
No. The reviewer must be someone other than the author.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="What happens to learners' records when I update a course?" >}}
Use **Start a new version**. Records from the earlier version are kept, and the old version shows as **Replaced** once the new one is published.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can Lumen draft the course for me?" >}}
You can start from a Clinical Educator module built in chat, with **Create a course from this module**. You still write, check and review the course before it's published.
{{< /faq >}}
