Renewal deadlines come around every two years, and the hours are scattered across certificates in your email. Lumen Learning keeps your licenses, the hours you've recorded and your completion records together, and shows **Due soon** before a renewal sneaks up on you.

{{< callout type="important" title="Being rolled out" >}}
Lumen Learning is being switched on gradually. If you don't see **Learning** in the sidebar, it isn't on for your account yet.
{{< /callout >}}

## Before you start

- **Learning switched on** for your account.
- **Your license details:** name, profession, state or jurisdiction, issuing board, cycle dates, and the hours your board requires.

## How to track a license

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open Learning" >}}Click **Learning** in the sidebar. Find **Licenses and certifications**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Add a license" >}}Click **Track a license or certification** and choose **License** or **Certification**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Fill in the details" >}}Enter the **Name**, **Profession**, **State or jurisdiction**, **Issued by**, **Cycle starts** and **Expires** dates, and the **Hours required this cycle**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Save it" >}}Save. The license shows as **Current**, **Due soon** (within 90 days of expiry) or **Expired**, with the hours you've recorded against the requirement you entered.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

## How to add education you took elsewhere

{{< steps >}}
{{< step title="Open your records" >}}Click **My records**.{{< /step >}}
{{< step title="Add it" >}}Under **Education taken elsewhere**, enter the **Title**, **Provider**, **Hours**, the **Credit, as on the certificate**, and the **Completed on** date.{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

These entries are self-reported. Keep the original certificates; Lumen doesn't store uploaded certificates.

## How to share your records

In **My records**, click **Share my records** and choose how long the link should last (1 to 365 days). Lumen shows the link once, so copy it then. Each Lumen completion record also has a **Copy verification link** that anyone can open to confirm it.

## Limits and safety

- **Lumen doesn't verify your board's rules.** The hours and dates are the ones you enter. Your board decides which hours count.
- **Self-reported entries are yours to back up.** Keep original certificates for anything you add under **Education taken elsewhere**.
- **Non-accredited records say so.** "This is a record of completion. It is not accredited continuing education credit."
- **Share links are public while they last.** Anyone with a share link can see the records it covers until it expires.

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Will Lumen remind me before my license expires?" >}}
Licenses within 90 days of expiry show **Due soon** and appear under **Needs your attention** on your Learning home page.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Does Lumen report my hours to my board?" >}}
No. Lumen keeps your records and lets you share them. Reporting to your board is up to you.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Can I upload a certificate?" >}}
Not currently. Record the details under **Education taken elsewhere** and keep the certificate yourself.
{{< /faq >}}
