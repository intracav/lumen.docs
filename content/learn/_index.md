You don't need to know how an AI model is built to use Lumen well. Knowing *why* it cites sources, *when* it reasons longer and *how* it decides to look something up makes you quicker to trust the right answers and to catch the wrong ones. Each page below explains one idea with a clinical analogy, shows how Lumen applies it, and is honest about the limits.

These pages use the general term **AI agent** when they explain the industry idea. In Lumen, agents are called **Specialists**.
