Lumen's organization plans add a shared layer on top of the personal assistant: your protocols and policies as first-class cited sources, departments with pooled usage, website bots grounded in your own content, and integration with your EHR. This section is the admin's map.

## What your team gets

- **[Departments, seats and pooled usage](/organizations/departments-and-seats/)** — mirror your real org chart (Organization → Department → Folder), let department heads run their own rosters, and share one pooled usage allowance instead of policing per-person quotas.
- **[Knowledge Packs you manage](/organizations/knowledge-packs-admin/)** — turn your protocols, reference PDFs, intranet pages, and even recorded in-services into cited, version-stamped answers for every member.
- **[Policies](/organizations/policies/)** — upload, version, and publish institutional policy so clinicians get a read-only Policy Library *and* policy citations directly in chat. Available as the qPolicy add-on on [Max](/plans/).
- **[Authority-source monitoring](/organizations/policy-monitoring/)** — register the CDC, FDA, CMS, and specialty-society pages your policies depend on, and get a reviewable finding when they change.
- **[Website Bots](/organizations/website-bots/)** — an embeddable chat widget for your public site that answers only from your Knowledge Packs, cites its sources, and captures leads.
- **[EHR integration](/organizations/ehr/)** — SMART on FHIR patient context and CDS Hooks advisory cards, read-only by design.

## Rollout in an afternoon

Bringing Lumen to a team is not an IT project. The honest version of the timeline:

<div class="steps-container">

{{< step num="1" title="Create your organization" >}}
Pick a plan on the [plans page](/plans/) — every organization plan includes five seats to start. You become the owner; add a co-admin if you want a second set of keys.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step num="2" title="Invite your people" >}}
Send email invitations, or hand department heads their own departments and let them invite their teams. Seats grow automatically as members join — no pre-purchasing spreadsheet required.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step num="3" title="Drop your protocols into a Knowledge Pack" >}}
Upload the PDFs your team actually uses — the vascular access protocol, the sepsis pathway, the escalation grid. Point Lumen at a URL or a recorded in-service if that's where the knowledge lives.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step num="4" title="Answers start citing YOUR content" >}}
From the next question a member asks, retrieval includes your pack — and when the answer comes from your protocol, the citation says so, with the document and version attached. The binder nobody opens becomes the source everybody sees.
{{< /step >}}
