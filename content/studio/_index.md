**Lumen Studio** is a video workspace inside Lumen. You write a short brief, such as "a two-minute explainer on flushing a midline for new graduate nurses", and Studio drafts it as an editable project: scenes, narration and on-screen text you can change. When it looks right, you export a video file.

{{< callout type="important" title="Studio is being rolled out" >}}
Studio is switched off by default and is being turned on gradually. If you open Studio and see **Studio is not available here yet**, it hasn't been switched on for your account. Nothing is wrong with your setup. Ask your Intracav contact if you'd like early access. The rest of this page describes what Studio does once it's on.
{{< /callout >}}

## Who Studio is for

- **Clinical educators** who need a short teaching video for orientation or a skills refresher.
- **Unit leaders** who want to explain a practice change in two minutes instead of a long email.
- **Device makers' training and marketing teams** who need product explainers they can keep editing as the product changes.

## How it works

{{< flow >}}
{{< flow-node kicker="1. Brief" title="Describe the video" icon="pencil" tint="blue" >}}Who it's for, what it should cover and how long it should be.{{< /flow-node >}}
{{< flow-node kicker="2. Project" title="An editable draft" icon="layers" tint="plum" >}}Studio writes the scenes. Each one stays editable.{{< /flow-node >}}
{{< flow-node kicker="3. Edit" title="Refine on a timeline" icon="sliders" tint="accent" >}}Change text, timing, graphics and clips.{{< /flow-node >}}
{{< flow-node kicker="4. Export" title="A video file" icon="download" tint="green" >}}Download it to share or post.{{< /flow-node >}}
{{< /flow >}}

The draft is a **project**, not a flat video. That matters: when a dose, a step or a product detail changes, you edit that one scene and export again, instead of starting over.

Once Studio is on for your account, it's in the sidebar under **More**, then **Studio**. The home page asks **What do you want to make?** Choose **Film**, describe the video, pick a **Style** such as Explainer or Technical, and click **Create video**. When you're happy with the edit, click **Export** and choose **Draft** (a quick, lighter file for checking the cut) or **Final** (full quality, takes longer). The result is an MP4 you can **Download**.

## What you can do in Studio

{{< card title="Draft a video from a brief" icon="film" tint="blue" >}}Describe the video in plain words. Studio plans the scenes, writes them and checks the result before handing you the project.{{< /card >}}

## When to use Studio, and when not to

| Use Studio for | Use something else for |
|---|---|
| A short explainer, orientation clip or product overview | A one-page handout or slide deck. Ask Lumen in chat instead, see [Artifacts](/chat/artifacts/) |
| Content you'll keep updating, because each scene stays editable | Live teaching with your audience in the room. Use [Lumen Live](/live/) |
| Drafting a video you'll review before it's shared | Anything that would publish without a person checking it first |

## Limits and safety

- **It isn't on for everyone.** Studio is being rolled out. Some accounts won't see it yet.
- **Creating and exporting needs a paid plan.** If your plan doesn't include Studio, you'll see **Studio is part of a paid plan**. You can still open a project someone shared with you. See [Plans](/plans/).
- **A daily limit.** Each person can start up to 12 new videos a day.
- **Recording needs a computer.** Recording isn't offered in the phone apps.
- **You review before you share.** Studio drafts content. It can be wrong. Check every clinical statement, dose and step against your sources and your organization's policies before you show the video to anyone.
- **No patient information.** Don't put identifiable patient details in a brief, a script or a recording.

{{< callout type="safety" title="Check clinical content before it's shared" >}}
A teaching video can spread a mistake to many people at once. Have someone qualified review the finished video against current policy and the product's instructions for use before you publish it.
{{< /callout >}}

## FAQ

{{< faq question="Why can't I see Studio?" >}}
Studio is switched off by default while it's being rolled out. If it hasn't been turned on for your account, Studio shows **Studio is not available here yet**. Ask your Intracav contact about access.
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Which plan do I need?" >}}
Creating, editing and exporting need a paid plan (Pro or above) once Studio is on for your account. Viewing a project doesn't need a plan. See [Plans](/plans/).
{{< /faq >}}
{{< faq question="Is the video locked once it's made?" >}}
No. Studio keeps your video as a project of separate scenes, clips and graphics. You can change any part and export again.
{{< /faq >}}
